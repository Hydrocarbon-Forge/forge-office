## Item 2 — Outside should be DAY, and the environment must fill the screen edges when zoomed out
Two parts.

### 2a. Day palette (currently twilight/sunset)
The round-4 vista is a dusk palette (sky `#232738` → `#F4B37D` golden horizon; dark ground `#1C2E24`).
The user wants a DAY look — still calming/nostalgic, but bright:
- Sky: bright daytime gradient, e.g. soft azure `#8FC7EA` top → pale warm haze `#EAF4DC` near the horizon.
- Add a soft sun (pale yellow disc + gentle glow) somewhere in the sky.
- Brighten the mountain layers (keep the layered parallax look but lighter, airy colors — e.g. distant
  peaks in soft blue-lilac `#9FB4D8`-ish, mid-ground hills in lighter greens).
- Ground/meadow: brighter greens (`#3E6B3F`/`#5C8A50`-ish range) instead of the dark `#1C2E24`.
- Water: brighter, sun-lit blues (`#4C86A8`-ish).
- Keep the overall cozy/nostalgic mood — bright and airy, not neon. Do a quick pixel-check on the baked
  vista: the top-of-sky color should be clearly a bright blue (B > R, brightness > 150).

### 2b. Environment fills the screen when zoomed out
Currently the pre-rendered vista canvas is exactly map-sized (44×40 tiles = 1408×1280px). When the user
zooms out (min 0.6×) to see the whole office, the visible area exceeds the map bounds and you see the
empty cleared background beyond the vista — the environment "ends" mid-screen.

Fix: the exterior environment must extend to the SCREEN edges at any zoom:
- In screen space (before the world transform is applied), fill the whole canvas with a background that
  continues the vista: a vertical gradient from sky color at the top to ground color at the bottom
  (horizon around ~45% down the screen), so there is NEVER a dead background at the screen edges.
- Also extend the pre-rendered vista canvas beyond the map bounds by a margin (e.g. +1024px each side)
  so trees/ground/water continue a bit past the map edges, and blit it with the corresponding offset.
- Keep the camera/viewport behavior EXACTLY as it is (the user explicitly wants the viewing window locked
  at its current degree, horizontally and vertically — do NOT change camera/zoom mechanics).

Verify: at the minimum zoom (0.6×), screenshot the full screen and confirm the screen edges show
environment (sky/ground/trees) rather than flat background — a pixel sample at each screen corner
should be vista-colored (bright sky or ground), not the clear color.

## Item 3 — Metrics window (drawStatusBoard) floats instead of pinned to the main area
Diagnosis (`web/render.js` `drawStatusBoard`): it's anchored to a fixed WORLD position
(`worldLx = 14.6*TILE`, `worldLy = 8.6*TILE`) but rendered via a screen-space override:
`ctx.setTransform(1,0,0,1,0,0)` with position `(worldX - camera.x) * zoom` and FIXED size `worldLw`
(no zoom scaling). Two problems:
1. The screen-space override sets the transform to raw device pixels, ignoring `this.dpr`. On a
   high-DPI screen (dpr=2) the window lands at HALF the intended position/scale — visually detached
   from its wall, "floating."
2. Position scales with `zoom` but size doesn't → as you zoom the window drifts off the wall it sits on.

Fix: pin it to its spot in the main area — render it in WORLD space like the other wall-mounted boards
(no screen-space override at all), so it stays glued to the wall, scales with zoom like everything else,
and is correct at any dpr. Keep it at the same world spot (the main area wall). If the constant world
size ends up too small at low zoom, you may keep a *size* that scales with the world (i.e. just draw it
in world units — it will scale naturally). The key requirement: it never detaches from the wall, at any
zoom, any dpr, any camera position.

Verify: headless — set a dpr of 2 (or mock it), and at two different zoom levels confirm the
drawStatusBoard output lands at the SAME world location (i.e. its on-screen position moves only with the
camera, exactly like a wall fixture). Screenshot at default and min zoom showing the board on its wall.

(capsule spans x -10..10, y -16..10). So the band floats ~9px above the head, and the earpiece
`(-11, -15, 3, 6)` sits high on the head instead of at ear height.

Fix: re-seat the headset on the head:

  lands at y ≈ -19.5 — resting ON the head top (-16) with slight overlap, not floating above it.
- Earpiece(s): move to ear height, at the side(s) of the head — roughly `(-13, -3, 4, 8)` (left cup,
  and/or a right cup at `(9, -3, 4, 8)`), i.e. y ≈ -3..5, x at/just outside the head edge (±10..±13).
- Boom mic: from the earpiece curving to just below the front of the visor (visor is x 0..13, y -10..-1;
  aim the mic tip around (2..4, 2..4)).
