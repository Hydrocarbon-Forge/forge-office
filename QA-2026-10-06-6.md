## Prioritized Issue List

### 1. [P1 - High] Glass Wall Raycast Attenuation Mismatch (Acoustics)
- **Location:** `web/voice.js` (`calculateWallOcclusion`), `web/map.js` (`T_WALL_GLASS`)
- **Severity:** P1 (High)
- **Description:** Glass meeting room walls are semi-transparent visually (`T_WALL_GLASS` with alpha transparency), and audio is completely isolated when the door is closed via server-side room containment. However, when the meeting room door is **open**, DDA raycasting treats `T_WALL_GLASS` identical to opaque reinforced concrete (`T_WALL`), dropping voice gain to `0.15` across 2 glass tiles.
- **Suggested Fix:** In `calculateWallOcclusion`, distinguish `T_WALL_GLASS` from solid opaque walls by assigning glass tiles a softer occlusion penalty (e.g. 0.75 per glass wall instead of 0.45) when the door is open.

---

### 2. [P2 - Medium] Whiteboard Mouse-Move Packet Throttling
- **Location:** `web/game.js` (`setupWhiteboardModal`)
- **Severity:** P2 (Medium)
- **Description:** During rapid continuous drawing on the glass whiteboard canvas, strokes are recorded and broadcast on mouse-up. However, if extended to live drawing stream or high-frequency input devices (120Hz/240Hz mice), point arrays can accumulate rapidly before `mouseup`.
- **Suggested Fix:** Add a distance threshold filter (e.g. discard points within 3px of previous point) and cap maximum points per individual stroke object to 100 before splitting into continuous stroke segments.

---

### 3. [P2 - Medium] Drink Station Add-ins Selection Retention on Base Switch
- **Location:** `web/game.js` (`setupDrinkStationModal`)
- **Severity:** P2 (Medium)
- **Description:** When switching base beverages in the Drink Station modal (e.g. switching from Coffee to Matcha or Fizzy Tonic), previously selected add-in checkboxes (such as Steamed Milk or Raw Sugar) remain checked. While humorous, mixing Steamed Milk into Fizzy Tonic is usually unintentional.
- **Suggested Fix:** Add recommended default add-ins per base or provide a "Reset Add-ins" affordance when changing the base beverage.

---

### 4. [P2 - Medium] Mobile Touch Drinking Affordance
- **Location:** `web/game.js`, `web/index.html` (`#mobileControls`)
- **Severity:** P2 (Medium)
- **Description:** On desktop, pressing `Q` or clicking the HUD carry pill (`[SIP: Q]`) triggers the 1s sipping animation. On mobile viewport touch mode, there is a virtual joystick and two action buttons (`SPRINT`, `ACTION [E]`), but no dedicated on-screen drink button. While tapping the HUD pill directly works, it is small on small smartphone viewports.
- **Suggested Fix:** On mobile viewports when `player.heldDrink` is not null, dynamically surface a floating `DRINK` action button next to the `ACTION [E]` button.

---

### 5. [P3 - Low] Projector Screen Presentation Banner Vertical Alignment
- **Location:** `web/render.js` (`drawMeetingRoomFurniture`)
- **Severity:** P3 (Low)
- **Description:** The wall-mounted projector screen housing (`37 * TILE, 15.2 * TILE`) sits cleanly on the north wall, but the upper shadow line extends slightly into the north wall baseboard border.
- **Suggested Fix:** Offset `sy` down by 2px to `15.28 * TILE` so the projector screen bracket aligns flush with the northern wall stud line.

---

### 6. [P3 - Low] Fallback Font for Beverage Emojis on Minimal Linux OS
- **Location:** `web/index.html` (`#vendingModal`, `#fridgeModal`), `web/game.js`
- **Severity:** P3 (Low)
- **Description:** Vending machine and fridge drink icons utilize standard Unicode emojis (`🥤`, `🍋`, `⚡`, `🍵`, `💧`, `☕`, `🫧`, `🧃`). On modern desktop and mobile browsers this renders with rich color, but on minimal headless Linux installs lacking `fonts-noto-color-emoji`, icons render as monochrome vector outlines.
- **Suggested Fix:** Add stylized 16×16 canvas-rendered sprites for the 8 default beverages as a crisp procedural fallback.

---

### 7. [P3 - Low] HUD Settings Button Hotkey Label Inconsistency
- **Location:** `web/index.html` (`#settingsBtn`)
- **Severity:** P3 (Low)
- **Description:** The top HUD buttons feature their shortcut keys: `MAP (B)`, `MIC (V)`, `CLOCK (K)`, `WARDROBE (C)`, while the settings button is labeled `⚙️ SETTINGS` without an associated quick key.
- **Suggested Fix:** Consider binding `O` (Options) or `,` (Preferences) as a keyboard shortcut for Settings, displaying `⚙️ SETTINGS (O)`.

---

### 8. [P3 - Low] Whiteboard Backdrop Opacity when Viewed from Hallway
- **Location:** `web/render.js` (`drawMeetingRoomFurniture`)
- **Severity:** P3 (Low)
- **Description:** The glass whiteboard is mounted on the west glass wall. When viewed from the outside corridor through the glass wall tiles, the mini whiteboard rendered in the world has a frosted white background (`rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.82)`). While visible, at lower zoom levels (0.6×) the standoff chrome corner pins blend into the wall frame.
- **Suggested Fix:** Add a 1px dark charcoal border (`#334155`) around the mini glass whiteboard boundary in world rendering.

---

### 9. [P4 - Trivial] Projector Screen Share Topic Field Sanitization
- **Location:** `web/game.js`, `server.js` (`projector-action`)
- **Severity:** P4 (Trivial)
- **Description:** Projector presentation topic strings are rendered exclusively via Canvas 2D `fillText`, which is inherently safe from XSS execution. However, entering leading/trailing spaces or extreme Unicode control characters is permitted by the server relay.
- **Suggested Fix:** Add a regex trim and printable character filter (`/^[a-zA-Z0-9 _\-\:\.]{1,32}$/`) in `server.js` for `projector-action`.

---

### 10. [P4 - Trivial] Coffee Machine Brew Steam Origin Offset
- **Location:** `web/game.js` (`brewCoffee`, `update(dt)`)
- **Severity:** P4 (Trivial)
- **Description:** During the 2-second espresso brew sequence, steam particles originate at `(4 * 32 + 16, 14 * 32 - 2)`. Depending on player facing angle when standing adjacent to the counter, the steam plume occasionally renders behind the player's head.
- **Suggested Fix:** In `render()`, ensure particle rendering pass executes after entities, or sort steam particle draw depth above avatars on row 14.
