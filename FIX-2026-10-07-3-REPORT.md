## Root Cause Diagnostics & Reality Comparison
- **Item 1 (Avatar Head Proportions)**:
  - *Spec diagnosis*: Stated that body top was at `y = -15` (from bezier `(0, -15)`).
  - *Actual codebase finding*: In `web/render.js` line 4041, the body bezier starts at `(0, -14)` (top at `y = -14`). The previous head was at `y = -14.5`, meaning the top of the head was flush with the body top (only 0.5px difference), creating the flat-top blob silhouette.
  - *Resolution*: Enlarged head to `drawRoundedRect(ctx, -7.75, -19.5, 15.5, 10.5, 4.5, true, true)`, placing the head top at `y = -19.5` (5.5px above body top `y = -14`) and bottom at `y = -9` (clean overlap with body, avoiding neck gaps). All facial features and hat bases were shifted up by 5.0px.
- **Items 2, 3, 4, 5**:
  - Codebase findings matched the specification exactly.
