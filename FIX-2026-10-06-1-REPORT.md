- **Root Cause Diagnosis Verification:**
  - **Diagnosed by C6:** Office was very dark at all times; ambient dark layer was a constant `rgba(14,18,28,0.76)` multiply in `web/map.js` `renderLightOverlay`.
  - **Our Verification:** Verified and confirmed. Line 476 hardcoded `rgba(14, 18, 28, 0.76)` regardless of client clock time.
- **Fix Implementation:**
  - Added `getAmbientDarkness(consoleOnline, lightsSetting)` in `web/map.js`:

    - Toggle turns darkness off entirely (ambient `0.0`, "not dark at all").
    - Key `N` toggles in-game; persisted in `localStorage` (`forge_office_lights_off`).
    - Added HUD button `💡 LIGHTS (N)` and HUD pill hint: `• N for Lights`.
    - Added checkbox on the Start Screen: `Full Brightness (Darkness Off)` with persistence.
  - Scaled light cutout intensity by daytime factor (`dayFactor = Math.min(1.0, ambientDark / 0.60)`) so lights are subtle by day and carve deep pools of light at night.
