## 1. Subagents appear when none are running
**Symptom:** tiny sub-agent blobs always present in harness offices (antigravity=2, others=1) even when nothing is actually running.
**Diagnosed root cause:** `web/agents.js` `initSubagents()` (line ~124-145) hardcodes the counts and never looks at live state.
**Fix:** drive subagent count from LIVE data. The console already exposes it: `GET /api/flow` → `{harnesses:[{id, name, running, active_sessions, subtasks, total_sessions, ...}]}` with ids `harness-opencode`, `harness-claude`, `harness-antigravity`, `harness-c6`. Add a `/api/flow` poll to `web/game.js` `setupDataPoller()` (same 15s cadence) and store it in `liveData.flow`. In `agents.js`, reconcile subagents on each poll: for each harness office, target count = `min(3, active_sessions + subtasks)` when `running` is true, else `0`. Animate spawns (pop-in) and despawns (walk out / fade). The deterministic brain can stagger their errands, but the COUNT must track the live signal. Map `harness-claude` → claude-code office, `harness-c6` → ignored for subagents (C6 has no office).
**Acceptance:** with the console's current data (all harnesses `running:false` except c6) the three offices show ZERO subagents; when a harness has `running:true` it shows >0.

## 2. Too dark — make lighting time-of-day based + a lights-off setting
**Symptom:** office is very dark regardless of real time; ambient dark layer is a constant `rgba(14,18,28,0.76)` multiply (web/map.js `renderLightOverlay`, line ~475-476).
**Fix:**
- Default: derive ambient darkness from the **real local time of day** (client clock): daytime (e.g. 7:00–19:00) ≈ ambient 0.10-0.15 (nearly fully lit, just a hint of mood), smooth transition at dawn/dusk, night ≈ 0.55-0.65. Lights (lamps, board glow, player auras) still carve holes at night, dimmer effect by day.
- Add a **"Lights" setting**: toggle that turns the darkness off entirely (ambient 0.0 — "not dark at all"). Key `N` (add to the HUD hint text) + a toggle on the start screen (persist in localStorage; `N` works in-game). Keep the console-offline dimming behavior as a small additional dimmer on top.
- Keep the flicker/glow look but scale it with the time-of-day factor so daytime is clearly bright.
**Acceptance:** at current real hour the scene is mostly bright; toggling the setting makes it fully bright; night hours get the darker mood.

## 3. Cannot enter claude-code's office — status board blocks the door

**Diagnosed root cause:** in `web/map.js` `buildBaseRooms()`, the status board is painted as SOLID `T_BOARD` across `x:27..37` at `y:10` (line ~115-118), and the tile directly in front of the door `(32,10)` is one of those solid board tiles — the player can never step onto the tile needed to pass through the door.
**Fix:** keep the board's visual position (lobby north wall) but leave a walkable gap at the door column: paint the board solid for `x:27..31` and `x:33..37`, leave `(32,10)` as lobby floor (render the board with a small gap around the door, or make the board wall-mounted/decorative and non-solid — your choice, but the door MUST be reachable). Re-check A* after the change: claude office reachable from the lobby spawn.
**Acceptance:** a player can walk from the lobby into the claude-code office through its door.

Show a small "E — talk" prompt when in range (like the box prompt). Keep the proximity auto-trigger too.
